Frequently asked questions

ToddlerCam is asking for Camera, Photo, and Microphone permission. Is that safe?

Yes. Camera access lets ToddlerCam show the live front and rear dual preview and take photos. Photo Library access lets it save your captures to your own device. Microphone access is requested by Apple's camera framework when configuring dual-camera video and is not used to record conversations. None of this data ever leaves your device — see our Privacy Policy for details.

I denied a permission by accident. How do I turn it back on?

Open the iOS Settings app → scroll down to ToddlerCam → toggle on Camera, Photos, and Microphone. You can also tap “Open Settings” from the in-app permission prompt.

How do I pair my Apple Watch as a remote?

Install the ToddlerCam companion app on your paired Apple Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone. Once both apps are open and your Watch shows “Connected,” you can trigger the shutter, start or stop video, swap cameras, and play attention-grabbing sounds right from your wrist.

How do I lock the phone to ToddlerCam so my toddler can't leave the app?

Tap the Guided Access badge on the camera screen for a step-by-step walkthrough, or go to Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access to enable it. Once on, triple-click the side button while ToddlerCam is open to start a locked session.

What is Toddler Lock and the Parental Gate?

Toddler Lock turns the screen into a safe touch canvas of bubbles and sounds without changing the camera. The Parental Gate is a simple math challenge that stands between your toddler and Settings, the photo Gallery, or any external links — so only a grown-up can get through.

Where are my photos and videos saved?

Directly to your device's own Photos library, the same place as your regular camera roll. ToddlerCam does not keep a separate copy and has no cloud storage of its own.

Does ToddlerCam work without an Apple Watch?

Yes — the Apple Watch app is an optional remote control. Every core feature, including dual-camera photo and video capture, works fully from the iPhone alone.
