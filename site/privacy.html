Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 9, 2026 · Developer: HEJI TECHNOLOGY LLC (com.hejitech.kidscam)

1. Introduction & Commitment to Child Safety

HEJI TECHNOLOGY LLC (“we”, “our”, or “us”) built ToddlerCam as a child-safe, privacy-by-design dual camera and parent remote control application designed specifically for toddlers, babies, and young children. We strictly respect the privacy of children and families.

ToddlerCam is designed with the following in mind:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K)
  • Apple's App Store Review Guidelines for the Kids Category (Guideline 1.3 & 5.1.4)

2. Zero Data Collection

ToddlerCam does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdates, locations, or identifiers.
  • No Accounts or Logins: The app functions entirely locally on your devices without requiring any account or registration.
  • No Third-Party SDKs or Trackers: There are no advertising frameworks, no tracking analytics (e.g. Firebase, AppsFlyer, Mixpanel), and no third-party SDKs embedded in ToddlerCam.
  • No Internet Transmission: Camera streams, photos, audio effects, and Watch communications never touch any external server or cloud service.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

A. Camera

Purpose: Used exclusively to display real-time video feeds from the front and rear cameras and to capture photos. Processing: All camera processing occurs entirely in real time on the device using Apple's AVFoundation framework. No video frames or facial recognition data are ever stored or uploaded.

B. Photo Library

Purpose: Allows the app to save captured dual photos and videos to your Apple Photos library, and allows parents in the Parent Zone to view recent photos or create Apple Watch photo faces. Security: Photos remain in your private device storage and Apple iCloud library (governed by your own Apple ID privacy settings). We have zero access to your photo library outside of the explicit, user-triggered save action.

C. Microphone

Purpose: Requested by system camera frameworks when configuring dual-camera video feeds and audio effects. ToddlerCam does not record ambient conversations or store audio recordings.

D. Apple Watch Connectivity

Purpose: Enables the Apple Watch companion app to communicate directly with the paired iPhone to trigger the camera shutter, play toddler attention-grabber sounds, and review the last photo thumbnail. Security: Communication occurs exclusively over Apple's encrypted local Bluetooth and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi channels.

4. Toddler Protection & Parental Gate

To ensure toddlers cannot accidentally navigate out of the app or change system settings:

  • Parental Gate: Access to Settings, Gallery, Guided Access tutorials, and external links requires passing an age-appropriate adult challenge (a simple math problem).
  • Guided Access Support: The app actively guides parents to configure iOS Guided Access to prevent young children from swiping home, switching apps, or pressing hardware buttons.

5. Advertising & In-App Purchases

ToddlerCam is completely ad-free. There are no banner ads, interstitials, rewarded videos, or sponsored content, and no behavioral targeting or commercial tracking of any kind.

6. Children's Privacy

ToddlerCam is listed in Apple's Kids Category and is intended to be operated by a parent or guardian on behalf of a child. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone — including children — there is no personal information from children for us to access, retain, or disclose.
