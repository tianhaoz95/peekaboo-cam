What's inside

Built for toddlers. Trusted by parents.

Every feature is designed so little hands can safely hold the phone while grown-ups stay in control.

🎥

Dual camera photo & video

Simultaneous front-and-rear capture in Picture-in-Picture, so you always keep both the moment and the reaction.

⌚️

Apple Watch parent remote

Trigger the shutter, start/stop video, swap cameras, and play silly attention-grabbing sounds — all from your wrist, without touching the phone.

🛡️

Guided Access walkthrough

A step-by-step tutorial helps you lock the phone to ToddlerCam using iOS Guided Access, so your child can't swipe away or open other apps.

🔒

Toddler-safe screen lock

Toddler Lock turns the screen into a bubble-popping touch canvas, and a parental math-gate keeps settings and the photo gallery adult-only.

🦁

Real-time face masks

Fun on-device Apple Vision face masks track head movement live — configured entirely from the Watch, so the toddler's screen stays clean.

🙈

Zero data collection

No accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs. Everything — camera feeds, face tracking, and photos — stays on your devices.
